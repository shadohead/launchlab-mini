TCRT5000 minimal stacked alternate v041

Open measured 31x13x1mm board cradle on the v033 launcher base. Two centered M2x4 screws secure a removable keeper/M5 dock, pressing on four bare PCB edge lands. Existing launcher latches are reused; the crossbar moves ahead of the larger board to clear solder. Tighten the sensor keeper through its reinforced driver windows first. Then attach the M5 underneath with two M2x6 screws at the accessible front overhang.

This is a design prototype, not a sliced print package. Electronics are references, never print them. Physical contact lands, wire envelope and optical alignment require fit checking. The TCRT and QRE versions remain separate.
